### Hello, I'm Virat

I build AI applications and the systems that make them usable: **agent tooling, observable infrastructure, machine learning, and data-driven products**. My public projects range from persistent MCP terminals and tracing dashboards to conversational apps and applied analytics.

I'm pursuing an **MS in Business Analytics & Artificial Intelligence at The University of Texas at Dallas**. I enjoy turning prototypes into things people can actually run, inspect, and improve.

**What I'm working on:** agent orchestration, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, observability for AI tools, browser/desktop automation, and practical local inference.
